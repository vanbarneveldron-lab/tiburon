THE OPPORTUNITY

Your business rarely stands still. Your outsourcing contract does.

Over time, organisations change. Teams grow or shrink, technology evolves and services that once made sense may no longer reflect actual demand.

That does not necessarily mean your IT provider is doing something wrong. It means there may be an opportunity to review the arrangement together and make it work better for everyone.

01

Contract vs. reality

Are you paying for services, capacity or minimum volumes you no longer use?

02

Cost transparency

Do pricing, changes, indexation and consumption remain understandable and proportionate?

03

Service alignment

Do service levels and technical choices still match the needs of the business?
